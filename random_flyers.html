Presentation Flow

The design is not random. The response should not be either.

01
Start with the decision

Before style, I focus on what the viewer needs to notice, feel, and understand first.

02
Build hierarchy that guides the eye

Headline, support message, brand cues, and CTA are arranged to remove hesitation and create flow.

03
Make the brand easier to trust

A good flyer should not just look good for a moment. It should make the offer feel stronger and easier to remember.
